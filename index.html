Our Story

Born in Muck City.Smoked with soul.

Buss It BBQ was built on a porch in Belle Glade — sugarcane fields on one side, Lake O on the other. Recipes passed through three generations of family pitmasters, slow-smoked over real wood, served with the kind of hospitality you only find south of the muck.

We're not chain BBQ. We're not fast BBQ. We're Florida BBQ — done right, every time.
